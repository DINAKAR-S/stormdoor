# stormdoor, explained simply

## What is it?

Your apps talk to AI. The AI **costs money** and **sometimes breaks**.

**stormdoor is a guard that sits in front of your AI.** Every request goes
through the guard first. The guard checks it, counts it, protects it, and can
even save you money.

Think of it like a **storm door**: the outer door that takes the bad weather so
your real door stays safe.

---

## The problem it stops

You have 4 apps all sharing **one** AI key.

One night, one app gets stuck and keeps asking the AI over and over.

- **Without a guard:** it spends **$400 by morning**. You find out from the bill.
  You can't even tell which app did it.
- **With stormdoor:** that app hits **its own $20 limit** and stops. The other 3
  keep working. In the morning you see exactly who spent what.

---

## What it can do (each one is a small superpower)

- **💰 Give each app a wallet**: set a spending limit. When the wallet is empty,
  that app stops. The others keep going.
- **🔑 Hand out fake keys**: apps never hold your real AI key. If one leaks,
  switch it off. Your real key never moves.
- **🧾 A receipt for everything**: see every question, which AI answered, and
  what it cost. One clear list.
- **🔀 A backup AI**: if one AI is down, use another one automatically. The user
  never notices.
- **💥 Break it on purpose**: pretend the AI failed and watch if your app copes.
  Like a fire drill, so the real bad day is boring. *(This is the special one no
  other tool does.)*
- **♻️ Remember answers**: same question again? Give the saved answer for free
  instead of paying twice.
- **🛡️ Hide private things**: if someone types a card number or email, wipe it
  before it ever leaves.
- **📈 Find the slow bits**: see where the time goes on each question.
- **🏷️ Bill each customer**: add up what each customer used and send it to your
  billing (like Stripe).
- **🔁 Pick up where it dropped**: if the internet dies mid-answer, reconnect and
  get the rest. No repeats, no gaps.

---

## What you actually see

One simple screen (a dashboard) with:

- Big numbers: questions today, money spent, money saved, secrets hidden.
- Each app with a **bar** showing how much of its wallet is used.
- A button that **breaks the AI on purpose** so you can watch it recover.
- A live list of recent questions:
  - **green** = it worked
  - **pink** = answer reused for free
  - **red** = an app hit its limit and was stopped (on purpose)

---

## Try it in 4 small steps

1. **Make a key** with a limit (its wallet).
2. **Point your app at it**: change 2 lines: the address and the key.
3. **Watch the screen**: questions, costs, and who is spending, live.
4. **Press "Break it"**: pretend the AI fails, watch your app stay standing.

---

## The whole point

So your AI apps never **overspend**, never **leak**, and never **fall over
quietly**, and you can **prove** they survive the bad day, because you can make
the bad day happen on purpose.
